Portfolio research methodology is embedded in server.js.

Core principles:
- Primary-source-first.
- Never invent data.
- Label reported data, management guidance, external estimates, calculations and assumptions.
- Use current web research.
- Analyse 5/10-year history and user-selected forward horizon.
- Review requested con-calls.
- Study moat, market share, customers, capacity, CAPEX, governance, legal matters, promoters and corporate actions.
- Use P/E, PEG, EV/EBITDA, DCF, Reverse DCF, SOTP, P/B/residual income and dividend methods where appropriate.
- Reverse DCF must identify what future performance is priced into today's market capitalisation.
- ROE/ROCE >20% is not a mandatory rule.
